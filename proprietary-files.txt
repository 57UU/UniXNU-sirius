# Firmware inputs of the sirius port: relative path | source | SHA-256 | consumer
#
# The port has no Mu-Silicium firmware and no boot.img. The two firmware-facing inputs are the
# device's U-Boot (the UEFI implementation) and the vendor partition that carries the payload;
# both come from sirius-distro and are flashed by the user, not built here.
#
# The Apple side is fetched from the IPSW by "unixnu ipsw fetch" into out/ipsw/21H461/ and is
# never committed: KernelCache, DeviceTree, StaticTrustCache and the Cryptex system trust cache.
#
# path | source | sha256 | consumer
# boot.img | https://github.com/57UU/sirius-distro (U-Boot 2026.07, EFI_LOADER) | <sha256> | ABL boot partition
# vendor (mmcblk0p80) | output of make-uboot-payload.py | <sha256> | U-Boot boot menu
